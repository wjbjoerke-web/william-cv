01 / Introduction

A focus on the coast.

I am a geology graduate from the University of Copenhagen, currently studying Environmental Sciences at La Rochelle Université, specialising in Coastal Geosciences and Geophysics.

My experience combines hydrological data work at GEUS with university fieldwork, marine measurements and coastal modelling. I am interested in seabed mapping, sediment dynamics and the use of data to understand coastal change.
